About Us

A growing community,

Mochi Web3 was founded on a single belief: Web3 education should be free, accessible, and honest. What started as a small group of traders became a 26,000-strong community built on real knowledge — no hype, no gatekeeping.

From beginner guides to advanced trading strategies, from curated airdrop hunts to live mentorship sessions — everything we do is for the community, by the community.
